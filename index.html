Offline AI ERP

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026
In short: Offline AI ERP does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No servers operated by us — the app works fully offline
  • The built-in AI assistant runs entirely on your device

Who we are

Offline AI ERP is a business management app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have any question about it, contact sivavimelrajhen@gmail.com.

Information the app stores

The app stores only what you enter or choose to add, so that it can run your business records:

  • Company details, logo, currency, tax rates and document settings
  • Employee, payroll, leave, attendance and settlement records
  • Assets, vehicles, suppliers, purchase orders and goods receipts
  • Clients, enquiries, quotations, invoices, receipts, projects and accounting entries
  • Photos you attach, such as employee pictures, asset images and your logo

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. iOS encrypts it at rest once the device has been unlocked after a restart. It is not visible to other apps and is not uploaded anywhere.

Information we collect

None. We do not receive any of your records, usage data, crash data, device identifiers or location. The app makes no network requests of its own.

The AI assistant

The AI model is included inside the app and runs on your device's processor. Your questions, your records and the answers it writes never leave the device.

Advanced users can point the app at an AI server they run themselves (a “custom endpoint”). This is off by default and only happens if you set it up in Settings. If you do, questions you send go to the server you configured. You can remove it at any time.

Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change any permission at any time in iOS Settings.

PermissionWhy it's used
CameraTo scan barcodes on employee ID cards, asset tags and vehicle labels. Nothing is recorded or saved.
PhotosTo add an employee photo, an asset picture or your company logo. Only the images you pick are used.
Microphone & SpeechTo dictate a question to the AI assistant. Recognition runs on your device.
Face ID / Touch IDTo unlock the app if you turn on App Lock. Biometric data stays with iOS and is never available to the app.

Sharing and exports

PDFs, CSV exports and backup files are created only when you ask for them. They leave your device only through the iOS share sheet, to a destination you choose, such as Mail, Files or AirDrop.

Backups

You can export a backup file at any time from Settings and restore it on another device. If your device itself is backed up to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Your control over your data

  • View and edit any record at any time in the app.
  • Export everything to a single backup file from Settings.
  • Erase all records from Settings → Data → Erase all data.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. We collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, any change will not affect information we hold — we hold none.
